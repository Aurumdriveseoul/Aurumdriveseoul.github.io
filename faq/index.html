Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp or Instagram, any time.

How much is a transfer from Incheon or Gimpo Airport to Seoul?

Standard is $120 and Premium is $180 per vehicle (launch offer), for destinations in Seoul within 60 km of the airport. See all prices and options.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. One price covers up to 4 passengers and up to 8 suitcases.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number and adjust the pickup time to your actual landing. The first 60 minutes of waiting are free.

Where will the driver meet me?

In the arrivals hall after customs, holding a sign with your name. We send the exact meeting point for your terminal before you land.

Do you pick up at Gimpo Airport too?

Yes. We serve both Incheon (Terminal 1 and 2) and Gimpo (international and domestic terminals). See Gimpo Airport transfer.

Do you run late at night?

Yes. Rides between 22:00 and 06:00 have a night supplement of $20.

Can we stop on the way to the hotel?

Yes. An additional stop within 10 km of the route is $20. Premium includes one stop, for example a convenience store or currency exchange.

Can I book the return trip to the airport?

Yes. Add the round-trip option (hotel ⇄ airport) for $70 when you book.

Does the driver speak English?

Yes, every ride comes with an English-speaking driver.

How do I book and pay?

Use the booking form, or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram (@aurumdrive.seoul). We confirm the price, pickup point and payment method with you before the ride.

We are more than 4 people. Can you help?

One vehicle seats up to 4. For larger groups, message us with your group size and we will tell you what we can arrange.

Landing soon?

Send your flight number and destination. We confirm your ride and your meeting point.
