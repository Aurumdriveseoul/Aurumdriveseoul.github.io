Where your driver meets you

After you collect your bags and pass customs, your driver waits in the arrivals hall with a sign showing your name. We send the exact meeting point for your terminal before you land.

Check whether your airline uses Terminal 1 or Terminal 2. They are separate buildings, so tell us the terminal when you book.

Flight delays and waiting time

We follow your flight number, so an early or late landing is not a problem. The first 60 minutes of waiting are free.

Seoul to Incheon Airport for your departure

Airlines usually recommend arriving 2–3 hours before an international flight. Tell us your flight time and we suggest a pickup time from your hotel.
