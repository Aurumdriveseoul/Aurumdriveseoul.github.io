Common questions

How much is a transfer from Incheon or Gimpo Airport to Seoul?

Standard is $120 and Premium is $180 per vehicle (launch offer), for destinations in Seoul within 60 km of the airport. See all prices and options.

Is the price per person or per vehicle?

Per vehicle. One price covers up to 4 passengers and up to 8 suitcases.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track your flight number and adjust the pickup time to your actual landing. The first 60 minutes of waiting are free.

Where will the driver meet me?

In the arrivals hall after customs, holding a sign with your name. We send the exact meeting point for your terminal before you land.

Do you pick up at Gimpo Airport too?

Yes. We serve both Incheon (Terminal 1 and 2) and Gimpo (international and domestic terminals). See Gimpo Airport transfer.
